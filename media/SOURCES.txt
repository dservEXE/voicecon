The scripts.d directory lists all dependent libraries, source URLs, revisions and patches.
FFmpeg --buildconf records the actual enabled components. GPL-only and nonfree components are disabled.
LGPL permits replacement and debugging of modified libraries; Voicecon imposes no restriction on this.
To use a compatible modified FFmpeg, place ffmpeg.exe and its shared DLLs in media-runtime next to Voicecon.exe.
No build of FFmpeg is downloaded while Voicecon is running. Automatic extraction uses only the embedded verified archive.
